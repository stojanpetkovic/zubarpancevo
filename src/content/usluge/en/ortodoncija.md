Straightening teeth is one of our specialities. We offer a range of options: **fixed braces** with classic brackets, **clear aligners** and **wire-free systems**, to give you straight, attractive teeth.

## An individual treatment plan

Every patient has their own needs and goals. The orthodontist approaches each one individually: detailed diagnostics, a treatment plan and monitoring of progress throughout treatment. We also take your aesthetic wishes into account, so that you are happy with your smile at the end.

## More than a beautiful smile

Orthodontics gives long-term results. Straightening the teeth and jaws improves not only how your smile looks, but also chewing, speech and oral health. Well-aligned teeth and jaws reduce the risk of excessive tooth wear, problems with the jaw joint (TMJ), problems with the tissue supporting the teeth, and teeth grinding.
