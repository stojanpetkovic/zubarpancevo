Our oral surgeons carry out routine and complex surgical procedures in the mouth, including implant placement. We also use PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin) technology, which improves healing and the success of implant treatment.

## Dental implants

An implant is a titanium screw placed in the jaw to replace a lost tooth. It is carefully placed in the bone, with which it then integrates. This creates a solid foundation for a denture, crown or bridge. The options, from a single implant to All-on-4 and All-on-6, are described on the [dental implants](/en/dental-implants/) page.

## PRF – faster, easier healing

PRF is a biological preparation made from the patient’s own blood, rich in growth factors and platelets. It is used during oral surgery to speed up wound healing, reduce the risk of complications and improve the quality of the tissue. The benefits are faster healing, less swelling and minimal discomfort during recovery.

## Surgical procedures

- tooth extraction and wisdom tooth surgery
- root-end resection (apicoectomy)
- sinus lift and bone augmentation (preparing the jaw for implants)
- removal of cysts and tumours
- placement of temporary implants

The combination of our oral surgeons’ experience and modern techniques shortens recovery and gives better long-term results.
