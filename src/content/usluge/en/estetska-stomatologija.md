Cosmetic dentistry corrects aesthetic flaws in a smile: discoloured teeth, irregular tooth shape and size, gaps or crowding, and prominent or uneven gums. We use a range of techniques to make your smile attractive and natural.

## Teeth whitening

Teeth whitening is one of the most common cosmetic treatments. We remove stains and discolouration using two methods, **in-practice** and **at-home whitening**, so you get the shade you want and a fresh smile.

## Tooth-coloured fillings

We use tooth-coloured fillings to repair damaged or decayed teeth and correct irregularly shaped teeth. Composite materials are matched to the natural colour of your teeth, so the fillings are invisible and look like a natural tooth.

## Ceramic veneers and crowns

Veneers are thin porcelain shells fitted to the front surface of the teeth to improve their shape, colour and appearance. We make veneers and crowns that look natural, last a long time and are tailored to each patient. For a complete smile makeover, see the [Hollywood smile](/en/dental-implants/#hollywood-smile).

We give cosmetic dentistry special attention, because a beautiful smile changes confidence and quality of life.
