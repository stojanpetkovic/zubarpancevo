Children’s and preventive dentistry looks after children’s oral health and prevents disease, so their smile stays healthy through childhood and beyond.

## An approach made for children

Dentists who work with children know how to talk to them and create a friendly, safe atmosphere during the visit. We adapt our approach and the treatment to the child, so they feel comfortable and unafraid. A start like this builds a positive relationship with the dentist from early childhood.

## Prevention

We teach children and parents good oral hygiene: how to brush, how to floss and which eating habits protect the teeth. Regular visits allow problems to be found early and preventive treatments such as fluoride application, fissure sealing and early treatment of decay.

We explain why baby teeth should be treated and how to prevent rampant decay in the article [Tooth decay in baby teeth](/en/tooth-decay-in-baby-teeth/).
