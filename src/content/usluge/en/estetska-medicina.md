As well as dentistry, we offer aesthetic medicine treatments. They focus on lifting the midface, fuller lips, the jawline, hydration and skin rejuvenation. The treatments are safe and tailored to each patient.

## Hyaluronic fillers

Fillers made of hyaluronic acid, a natural substance found in our skin, fill wrinkles, add volume to the lips and lift certain areas of the face, while keeping a natural look and facial harmony.

## Mesotherapy cocktails

Mesotherapy means injecting a cocktail of nutrients, vitamins, minerals and antioxidants directly into the skin. It improves hydration, texture and skin tone, giving the skin a healthy, radiant look.

## PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

PRP uses growth factors from the patient’s own blood to stimulate natural tissue regeneration. On the skin, it improves texture, reduces wrinkles and scars, and stimulates hair growth.

## Botox

Botox reduces expression lines on the forehead, around the eyes and between the eyebrows by relaxing the muscles that cause them. Correctly dosed, it leaves the skin smooth while your expression stays natural.

## Face and body lipolysis

Lipolysis with mesotherapy cocktails breaks down fat deposits in targeted areas and shapes the face and body without surgery, while improving skin texture.
