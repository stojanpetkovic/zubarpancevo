If you have lost some or all of your teeth, we offer removable and fixed prosthodontics to replace them and restore both appearance and function. We also make implant-supported restorations, a permanent solution for missing teeth.

## Removable prosthodontics

Removable prosthodontics replaces teeth for patients who have lost all or most of them. It means making a partial or complete denture, depending on the case. A partial denture is used when the patient still has some of their own teeth, a complete denture when they have none. You can take a removable denture out and put it back in.

## Fixed prosthodontics

Fixed prosthodontics means restorations that are permanently attached in the mouth: crowns and bridges. Crowns replace damaged, weakened or single missing teeth and restore their function and appearance. A bridge is made when several teeth need replacing, with the existing teeth acting as supports.

Inlays and onlays are restorations for smaller areas of damage, usually made of porcelain.

## Implant-supported prosthodontics

Implant-supported prosthodontics combines the restoration with dental implants. An implant is a titanium screw placed in the jaw where a tooth has been lost. Once it has integrated with the bone, a crown or bridge is fitted on it. It is a stable, permanent solution for missing teeth. Read more on the [dental implants](/en/dental-implants/) page.

We tailor every restoration to the patient, with thorough diagnostics and planning, so that it looks good, works well and feels comfortable.
